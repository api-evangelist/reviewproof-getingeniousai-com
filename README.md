ReviewProof, powered by Ingenious AI, provides businesses with audit‑grade provenance verification for online reviews. It helps local businesses and agencies demonstrate that their customer feedback is authentic, complying with FTC regulations on fake and AI‑generated reviews. The platform imports reviews, analyses risk signals, and generates detailed provenance certificates and widgets that can be displayed publicly. Pricing includes a free tier for a single location and paid plans for professional and agency use, offering multi‑location support, evidence exports, and AI‑drafted response suggestions.

**Areas:** Company · Review · Provenance · Fraud · FTC
